Privacy & storage

Photos, edits, thumbnails and feedback stay on this backend. Photo deletion removes managed shot files; feedback and evidence logs have separate retention. Sharing and Save to Photos create copies outside Autopilot; deleting here cannot remove those copies.

Retention applies to local managed captures. Original camera-card files are unaffected.

Camera card (read-only)

Browse files on the selected camera card, including photos taken without this app. Listing and import are read-only — this page never deletes from the card. Full-resolution zoom opens a local JPEG copy; RAW/ARW can be imported but is not shown as a photograph here.

Choose the exact camera on the Camera page first.
